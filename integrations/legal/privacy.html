LEGAL / 02 — DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This notice explains the customer and technical information Sky_Hex Integrations may use to set up, operate, troubleshoot and bill for integrations and monitoring services.

PROVIDER REVIEW NOTE: The legal provider has reviewed the service and data-handling information used to prepare this notice. The provider should keep the described data flows, provider terms, lawful-basis records, retention periods and security practices under review and update this notice if the live service changes.

1. Who is responsible for your information

The data controller is the legal provider of Sky_Hex Integrations. The provider's full legal identity and postal address are set out in the Terms & Conditions. For privacy questions or requests, email sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

We aim to collect only information relevant to the service you request. Depending on the integration, this may include:

  • Your contact email address and correspondence.
  • Your Discord username, user ID, server name and server ID, only where needed for the requested feature.
  • Webhook URLs, tokens, destination details and customer-specific configuration needed to make the integration work.
  • Service-specific information such as server names, status, player counts and activity events, only where required by the agreed integration.
  • Operational logs, event history, delivery results, error messages and timestamps where needed for the service, troubleshooting or security.
  • Messages or embeds generated and sent by the integration. The integrations do not access or collect existing Discord message content as a general feature.
  • Limited billing and subscription information available through Stripe, such as customer or subscription identifiers, plan, invoice, payment and subscription status. Stripe processes payment-card details; we do not ask customers to send card details to us.

The current Stripe checkout also asks for an email address and offers optional fields for a Discord username and a Discord server invite link. If you provide these optional details, we use them to identify your order and coordinate the requested setup. A server invite link may grant access to a Discord server, so do not provide one unless you are authorised to do so and it is needed for setup. Where possible, we will arrange installation permissions separately and avoid retaining invite links longer than necessary.

The precise information depends on the service agreed with you. The free discussion, specification, design and testing stage takes place before a paid subscription is formed and carries no obligation to subscribe. Information supplied at that stage is used to assess, prepare and test the proposed integration. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, recovery codes or unrelated sensitive personal information.

3. Why we use it and lawful bases

  • Pre-subscription enquiries, specifications and design/testing: to take steps at your request before deciding whether to enter a contract.
  • Providing the paid integration and customer support: to perform the subscription contract and take steps requested by you.
  • Subscription administration and billing: to perform the contract, including checking subscription and payment status through Stripe.
  • Tax, accounting and legally required records: to comply with legal obligations that apply to the sole-trader provider.
  • Service security, access control, fault investigation and abuse prevention: where appropriate, the provider's legitimate interests in protecting the service, customers and systems. The provider must assess that these interests are not overridden by the individual's rights and reasonable expectations.

Where an integration processes Discord member or player information on a customer's instructions, the customer may be the controller and Sky_Hex may act as a processor for that activity. The roles and lawful basis depend on the actual purpose and decisions for the particular integration and must be documented in the Service Specification and, where applicable, a data processing agreement. The provider must keep a record of the lawful basis used for each processing purpose. No single lawful basis should be assumed to cover every activity.

4. Service providers, recipients and international processing

  • IONOS: hosts the VPS used to run integration scripts and customer-specific configuration. The VPS is located in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Discord: receives integration-generated messages, embeds and webhook content and provides the Discord platform functionality required by the service.
  • Stripe: provides hosted checkout, subscription billing and the Customer Portal. The provider and authorised business users can access customer email addresses, subscription status, invoices and payment history through the Stripe Dashboard. Payment-card data is handled by Stripe and is not routinely downloaded or stored by Sky_Hex.
  • Customer-authorised webhook destinations: if requested, information may be sent to a webhook URL supplied by the customer. The customer must confirm authority to use that destination and approve the specific data fields. Only the agreed fields should be transmitted. The destination's operator may process the data under its own terms.
  • Google Analytics: used to measure website usage. The live configuration, data collected, cookie behaviour and available controls must be checked and described accurately; this notice does not claim that Google Analytics is cookie-free.
  • Google Fonts and embedded Stripe content: may receive technical browser information when the relevant external resources are loaded.

The legal provider should check the current contracts, data-processing terms, subprocessors and actual processing locations for IONOS, Stripe, Discord, Google and any customer-requested destination. The VPS is in Germany; transfers to Germany are generally covered by the UK's adequacy regulations, but this does not remove other UK GDPR obligations. Other provider locations and transfer safeguards must be assessed separately.

5. Security and access

Integration scripts and customer-specific configuration are hosted on an IONOS VPS in Frankfurt, Germany. The provider reports using SSH key authentication, disabling password-based SSH login and direct root SSH login, administering through a sudo-enabled account, enabling a firewall and Fail2Ban, regularly installing Ubuntu security updates and Python dependency updates, and receiving alerts for successful SSH logins. Webhook URLs and tokens are kept in separate configuration files with restricted permissions rather than embedded directly in Python scripts.

Direct VPS access is currently restricted to the integration operator. Stripe Dashboard access is held by the integration operator and the legal provider. Local legal documents containing customer email addresses and signatures are stored on an encrypted computer accessible to both, using a shared Windows login. The provider should improve individual account separation where practicable.

Automatic VPS backups are currently disabled. The provider should understand the resulting recovery risk and consider an encrypted, access-restricted backup and restoration plan. No security measure guarantees complete security. Customers should keep webhook credentials private and notify us promptly if they believe a credential has been exposed.

6. How long information is kept

  • VPS operational data and configuration: retained while the relevant subscription is active. When a customer cancels, the integration is disabled or removed and the operator manually deletes the customer's operational files and configuration from the VPS as soon as reasonably practicable. This does not delete copies already held by Discord, Stripe or a customer-authorised webhook destination.
  • Customer contracts and signed legal documents: stored locally on an encrypted computer and intended to be retained for up to six years after the customer relationship ends where necessary for contractual claims, disputes or legal obligations. The provider should review whether each record remains necessary and securely delete it when the retention purpose ends.
  • Stripe customer, invoice, subscription and payment records: remain in Stripe after cancellation for as long as required for accounting, tax, legal and dispute purposes. The provider should apply the relevant HMRC record-keeping period and review records when they are no longer required.
  • Pre-subscription enquiries and specifications: retained only for as long as reasonably needed to assess and prepare the proposed service, and deleted or anonymised when no longer needed if the customer does not proceed.
  • Backups: automatic VPS backups are currently disabled. If backups are introduced, the provider must document their location, access controls and deletion cycle.
  • Google Analytics: the applicable retention period depends on the live Analytics configuration and must be checked and set in the Analytics account.

Retention periods are maximum intended periods, not a reason to keep information that is no longer needed. Records may be retained longer where a specific legal obligation or active dispute requires it, with access restricted to those who need them.

7. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and data portability. To make a request, email sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are concerned about how your information is handled.

8. Website analytics, cookies and external content

Sky_Hex Integrations does not use Google Analytics for website visitor analytics. The plans page embeds Stripe's hosted pricing table and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those third-party services may receive technical information from your browser when the relevant content or resources are loaded and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. The provider should review the live website when scripts or embedded services change and provide any additional notice or consent required by applicable law.

9. Updates

This notice must be updated before any new category of information, purpose or recipient is introduced. The current version should be provided when information is collected, not merely left for customers to discover.
