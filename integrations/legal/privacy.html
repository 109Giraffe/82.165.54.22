LEGAL / 02 — DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This notice explains the customer and technical information Sky_Hex Integrations may use to set up, operate, troubleshoot and bill for integrations and monitoring services.

DRAFT FOR REVIEW: These policy pages are working drafts prepared for Sky_Hex Integrations. Before accepting orders, the adult legally responsible for the business must confirm the contracting business/legal name, postal address, service terms, data practices and cancellation arrangements, and obtain appropriate legal/accounting advice. Do not treat this draft notice as a substitute for those details.

1. Who is responsible for your information

The data controller is the legal provider of Sky_Hex Integrations. The provider's full legal identity and postal address are set out in the Terms & Conditions. For privacy questions or requests, email sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

We aim to collect only information relevant to the service you request. Depending on the integration, this may include:

  • Your contact email address and correspondence.
  • Your Discord username, user ID, server name and server ID.
  • Webhook URLs, destination details, API identifiers and configuration values needed to make the integration work.
  • Service-specific information such as server names, status, player counts or activity events, where those are part of the requested function.
  • Technical diagnostics such as delivery results, error messages, timestamps and limited operational logs needed to monitor and troubleshoot the service.
  • Limited billing and subscription information supplied by Stripe, such as customer or subscription identifiers, plan and payment status. Payment card details are handled by Stripe; we do not need or ask you to send card details to us.

The precise information depends on the service agreed with you. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, recovery codes or unrelated sensitive personal information.

3. Why we use it

  • To discuss your requirements, create and configure your integration.
  • To deliver webhook messages, notifications, monitoring and other agreed functions.
  • To provide support, diagnose faults, maintain security and prevent misuse.
  • To administer subscriptions, confirm payment status and respond to billing queries.
  • To keep records required for legal, accounting and dispute-resolution purposes.

Depending on the activity, the relevant lawful basis may be taking steps at your request or performing a contract, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or compliance with a legal obligation. The responsible operator must confirm and document the lawful basis for each use.

4. Who may receive information

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the service, including:

  • Stripe: hosted checkout, subscription billing and the Customer Portal.
  • Discord: where your requested integration uses Discord accounts, servers, bots, messages or webhooks, and where you contact us for support through Discord.
  • IONOS: hosting infrastructure used to operate relevant Sky_Hex systems.
  • Your chosen webhook destination or platform: data is sent there when required by the integration you request.

These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Their involvement and the exact data shared depend on the service. Provider locations and international transfer safeguards must be checked by the responsible operator before launch.

5. Webhook and configuration security

Webhook URLs can contain secret tokens. We use them only as needed to configure and operate the agreed integration, and access should be limited to people who need it. You should share them only through an agreed channel and rotate or revoke them if exposed. Do not include private credentials in public Discord channels or support messages.

No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. The service operator should maintain appropriate access controls, backups and security practices for the systems it operates.

6. How long information is kept

Customer-specific operational data, integration configuration and service logs are intended to be kept only while the customer's subscription is active. When a subscription ends, we will disable the integration and delete or anonymise operational data and configuration that is no longer needed as soon as reasonably practicable. Some information may need to be retained separately for a justified period to meet legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution or security obligations. Enquiry and specification information supplied before a subscription starts may be retained only for as long as reasonably needed to discuss, prepare and test the proposed integration, and will be deleted or anonymised if the customer does not proceed and there is no continuing reason to keep it. Backups may take a reasonable time to expire under the documented backup cycle. The provider must set and follow actual deletion periods for each category before launch.

7. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and data portability. To make a request, email sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are concerned about how your information is handled.

8. Cookies and external content

The plans page embeds Stripe's hosted pricing table and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those third-party services may receive technical information from your browser when their content is loaded and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. The site operator should check the live cookie behaviour and provide any additional notice or consent required before launch.

9. Updates

This notice must be updated before any new category of information, purpose or recipient is introduced. The current version should be provided when information is collected, not merely left for customers to discover.
