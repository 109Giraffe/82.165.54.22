LEGAL / 03 — BILLING & CANCELLATION

Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how to cancel a Sky_Hex Integrations subscription, request a refund and exercise applicable consumer cancellation rights.

DRAFT FOR REVIEW: These policy pages are working drafts prepared for Sky_Hex Integrations. Before accepting orders, the adult legally responsible for the business must confirm the contracting business/legal name, postal address, service terms, data practices and cancellation arrangements, and obtain appropriate legal/accounting advice. Do not treat this draft notice as a substitute for those details.

1. Managing a subscription

Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. Existing customers can use the Stripe Customer Portal to view billing details and manage or cancel their subscription. You can also contact sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com for help.

Subscriptions renew and are charged monthly. A customer may cancel a rolling monthly subscription at any time through the Stripe Customer Portal. Under the ordinary subscription arrangement, cancellation stops future renewals and access to the subscription service ends immediately, even if time remains in the current paid month. Unused time is not automatically refunded. This ordinary subscription cancellation is separate from the statutory 14-day cancellation right that may apply to a consumer's online service contract, which is explained below. Nothing in this policy limits a statutory cancellation or refund right.

2. Statutory cancellation rights

If you are a consumer buying online, you generally have a statutory 14-day period in which to cancel a service contract, starting the day after the contract is made, subject to the detailed rules and exceptions in law. This right is different from cancelling a monthly renewal and is not removed by using the Stripe Customer Portal.

Because custom setup may begin after payment, we must not rely on payment alone as permission to start supplying the service during the 14-day period. If you want work to begin before that period ends, the checkout or order process must capture your express request to start early and the required acknowledgement. If you then cancel during the period, a proportionate amount may be payable for service actually supplied before cancellation where the legal conditions are met. A consumer does not automatically lose the cancellation right merely because custom work has started; loss of the right after full performance requires the statutory conditions and the required acknowledgement. These steps must be implemented in the ordering process before launch.

3. How to request a cancellation or refund

Use the Stripe Customer Portal to cancel future monthly renewals. To exercise a statutory cancellation right, request a refund or discuss a problem with the service, email sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com with the account email, relevant Stripe invoice or subscription reference, and a short explanation. You do not have to use a particular form of words to exercise a statutory right to cancel. Do not email full card details.

We will review the request, check the payment and service history, and respond by email on a best-efforts basis; no fixed response time is guaranteed. Where a refund is due, it will normally be sent through the original payment method using Stripe. The time for the funds to appear can depend on the payment provider and bank.

4. When a refund may be appropriate

Refund requests are considered in line with applicable law and the circumstances, including duplicate charges, payments taken in error, a service not supplied as agreed, or other circumstances where a refund is legally required or otherwise appropriate. We do not use a blanket “no refunds” rule to remove statutory rights.

If a service is affected by a third-party outage or a customer-controlled system, we will look at the specific circumstances and the service agreed. This does not remove any rights the customer has under consumer law.

5. Cancellation form

If you wish to cancel a consumer service contract within a statutory cancellation period, you can use the following wording and email it to us. You do not have to use this exact wording if your message clearly tells us that you wish to cancel.

Cancellation message
To: Sky_Hex Integrations — sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com
I/We hereby give notice that I/We cancel my/our contract for the following service: [service/plan].
Ordered on: [date].
Customer name: [name].
Customer email: [email].
Date sent: [date].

The legal provider's identity and postal address are set out in the Terms & Conditions. The provider must ensure this information is also supplied in the required pre-contract and cancellation information at checkout.
