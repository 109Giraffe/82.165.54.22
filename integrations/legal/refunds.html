LEGAL / 03 — BILLING & CANCELLATION

Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how to cancel a Sky_Hex Integrations subscription, request a refund and exercise applicable consumer cancellation rights.

Last reviewed: 7 November 2026.

1. Managing a subscription

Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. Existing customers can use the Stripe Customer Portal to view billing details and manage or cancel their subscription. You can also contact sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com for help.

Subscriptions renew and are charged monthly. A customer may cancel a rolling monthly subscription at any time through the Stripe Customer Portal. If the Customer Portal is unavailable or the customer needs help, they can email sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com. Under the ordinary subscription arrangement, cancellation stops future renewals and access to the subscription service ends immediately, even if time remains in the current paid month. We will disable or remove the integration from the connected Discord server(s) as soon as reasonably practicable. Unused time in the current paid month is not automatically refunded, subject always to statutory rights and any refund required by law. This ordinary subscription cancellation is separate from the statutory 14-day cancellation right that may apply to a consumer's online service contract, which is explained below. Nothing in this policy limits a statutory cancellation or refund right.

2. Statutory cancellation rights

The free discussion, specification, design and testing stage takes place before a paid subscription contract is formed. It is free of charge and carries no obligation to subscribe. For a consumer buying the paid subscription online, the statutory 14-day cancellation period generally starts the day after that subscription contract is made, subject to the detailed rules and exceptions in law. The free pre-subscription stage does not start that 14-day period. This right is different from cancelling a monthly renewal and is not removed by using the Stripe Customer Portal.

The paid service is supplied after checkout, when we manually install or connect the integration and make it available. If that service is to begin before the 14-day cancellation period ends, the order process must obtain the consumer's express request for early performance and the required acknowledgement. If the consumer then cancels during the period, a proportionate amount may be payable for service actually supplied before cancellation where the legal conditions are met. A consumer does not automatically lose the cancellation right merely because service has started; loss of the right after full performance requires the statutory conditions and the required acknowledgement. The checkout and order process must be configured to capture these choices correctly before launch.

3. How to request a cancellation or refund

Use the Stripe Customer Portal to cancel future monthly renewals. To exercise a statutory cancellation right, request a refund or discuss a problem with the service, email sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com with the account email, relevant Stripe invoice or subscription reference, and a short explanation. You do not have to use a particular form of words to exercise a statutory right to cancel. Do not email full card details.

We will review the request, check the payment and service history, and respond by email on a best-efforts basis; no fixed response time is guaranteed. Where a refund is due, it will normally be sent through the original payment method using Stripe. The time for the funds to appear can depend on the payment provider and bank.

4. When a refund may be appropriate

Refund requests are considered in line with applicable law and the circumstances, including duplicate charges, payments taken in error, a service not supplied as agreed, or other circumstances where a refund is legally required or otherwise appropriate. We do not use a blanket “no refunds” rule to remove statutory rights.

If a service is affected by a third-party outage or a customer-controlled system, we will look at the specific circumstances and the service agreed. This does not remove any rights the customer has under consumer law.

5. Cancellation form

If you wish to cancel a consumer service contract within a statutory cancellation period, you can use the following wording and email it to us. You do not have to use this exact wording if your message clearly tells us that you wish to cancel.

Cancellation message
To: Sky_Hex Integrations — sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com
I/We hereby give notice that I/We cancel my/our contract for the following service: [service/plan].
Ordered on: [date].
Customer name: [name].
Customer email: [email].
Date sent: [date].

The legal provider's identity and postal address are set out in the Terms & Conditions. The provider must ensure this information is also supplied in the required pre-contract and cancellation information at checkout.

Billing, cancellation and refund enquiries: sky.hexbusiness@gmail.com.
